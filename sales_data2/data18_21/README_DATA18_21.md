# data18_21 — the initial dataset (2018–2021, 12 branches)

The first four years of Glow Beauty as the warehouse sees them: two normal years, then the two COVID
years. This folder is what the **initial load** (`ETL_Process\initial_loading\`) is built from.

Generated by [../gen_sales_data2.py](../gen_sales_data2.py) together with the other two folders —
see [../README.md](../README.md) for the full pattern list and the branch table.

## COVID in this folder

- product / service demand factors per phase, with the Klang Valley treated separately for the
  Oct 2020 CMCO and the 3–16 Jul 2021 EMCO — see [../README.md](../README.md)
- **staff cuts**: 25 junior staff resign in Apr–Jun 2020 (16) and Jul–Sep 2021 (9) (their last salary rows are
  2020-03..06 and 2021-06..08); managers and senior therapists stay; the rehiring wave is in data22_23
- pay −20 % Apr–May 2020, −15 % Jun–Aug 2021; 13th-month and Raya bonuses halved in 2020 and 2021;
  no increment in Jan 2021; EPF employee deduction 11 % → 7 % (Apr–Dec 2020) → 9 % (Jan 2021 on)
- rent −30 % in Apr–May 2020 and Jun–Aug 2021; electricity / water / maintenance near zero while closed
- new registrations drop to ~35 % during closures; the basket shifts to cleansers and masks

## Other patterns

- **2018 tax holiday**: `order_tax` and `serv_tax` are 0.00 from 1 Jun to 31 Aug 2018 (GST zero-rated,
  SST from 1 Sep), 6 % everywhere else
- weekday-morning 5 % off on services; loyalty discounts Bronze 0 / Silver 3 / Gold 7 / Platinum 12 %
- 15 % promo and bigger baskets on 11.11 / 12.12 / 3.3 / 9.9 / 10.10
- salaries +3 % in Jan 2019, 13th month every December, Raya bonus in the Raya month
  (2018-06, 2019-06, 2020-05, 2021-05)

## Data quality (checked by `gen_sales_data2.py --verify`)

Zero orphans; no order or reservation before the customer's registration or the branch's opening;
serving staff always at the transacting branch and employed on the day; reservations only by
therapists, never double-booked, all within 10:00–20:00; no salary outside the employment window.

## Loading into Oracle

Dates are `YYYY-MM-DD`; timestamps `YYYY-MM-DD HH24:MI:SS`; money has two decimals; plain ASCII, no
BOM. The control files in `operational_DB\sqlloader_control_files\` set the formats per column:

```
cd operational_DB\sqlloader_control_files
load_all.bat dwh <password> XE                      (this folder is the default)
```

Then `dwh\create_dwh.sql`, `initial_load_date_dim.sql` (2018-01-01..2021-12-31),
`gen_holidays.py 2018 2021`, `init_dimension\01..07`, `init_fact\01..05`,
`validate_initial_loading.sql`. Full walk-through: [../../LOADING_GUIDE.md](../../LOADING_GUIDE.md) Part A.
