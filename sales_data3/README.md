# sales_data3 — Glow Beauty operational data, 2018–2025, 13 branches (revision 3)

The complete source history of the business, generated by **one script from one world model** and
split into three load folders so the warehouse can be built once and then extended twice:

> **Revision 3 of the dataset.** `sales_data2` (revision 2) halved the order volume to fit Oracle XE
> but kept payroll, rent, stock buying and basket size at full scale, so **every branch lost money in
> every year** (FY2024 salary alone was 91 % of revenue). Revision 3 keeps the same world, seed, rows
> and IDs - every count, ID range and monthly table below is unchanged, and `sales_data2` and
> `sales_data3` differ **only in the amount columns** of `order_detail` (quantity, hence discount and
> tax), `purchase` (quantity, unit cost), `salary_payment` (base, bonus, deduction) and
> `branch_expense` (amount). Levers, all chosen so they consume the same random draws as revision 2:
>
> - order-line quantity 1-5 (was 1-3): 2.37 units/line on a plain day, **2.68 in a festival run-up**
>   (gifting before CNY / Raya / Deepavali / Christmas), **2.92 on 11.11 / 12.12-type mega-sale days**
> - supplier cost 33-43 % of the shelf price (was 45-55 %)
> - restock = 1.08 x units sold + 0-2 safety units (was 1.15 x + 5-15, i.e. ~1.5 x what was sold)
> - salary bands ~22 % lower (managers / leads ~28 %); a 2025 junior still earns >= RM 1,700
> - base rent 30 % lower (KL 12.95k ... Gombak 4.85k in 2018, +3 %/year)
>
> Result - the P&L now tells a coherent story: a young chain close to break-even in 2018-19, MCO
> losses in 2020-21, profitable from 2022 and ~10 % margin by 2024-25; in FY2024 12 of 13 branches
> are in the black (Ipoh, in its second year, is not; Melaka is the weakest at about 0 %):
>

Every folder carries the same 14 file names and byte-identical headers, so the 14 SQL\*Loader control
files in `operational_DB\sqlloader_control_files\` (all `APPEND`) load any of them unchanged. IDs
start at 1 in `data18_21` and continue across folders. Reference tables that add nothing in a folder
are header-only files (0 rows loaded — correct, not an error).

The files follow the project ERD: `staff.csv` has one job-title column `st_position` (Branch Manager
/ Senior Therapist / Beauty Therapist / Sales Assistant / Receptionist / Cashier — no role, no
salary column; salaries live in `salary_payment.csv` — 2018 bands: manager RM 4.2–4.8k, senior therapist
2.6–3.1k, therapist 1.9–2.3k, juniors 1.45–1.9k, +3 %/year), `order_detail.csv` has **no unit price** (a
line is priced at the product's price of its date — the base price, the 2023 price or the 2025 price
— so `order_tax` = 6 % of `qty × price − discount` lets you recover it), and `reservation.csv` has
`booking_date` (when the customer booked, 0–14 days ahead) **and** `reservation_date` (the
appointment day, which is what the warehouse dates the visit by).

```
python gen_sales_data3.py            # regenerate all three folders (seeded, ~1 min) + verify
python gen_sales_data3.py --verify   # only re-check what is on disk
```

> `..\sales_data\` (data / data2 / data3) is the previous generation of this dataset — 5–6 branches,
> 2019–2025, no single generator. It is kept for reference and cannot be loaded into the same OLTP as
> this tree (both start their IDs at 1).

## Patterns built in

**COVID-19 (Malaysian timeline).** Product and service demand carry separate factors; the Klang
Valley (Selangor + WP) is treated differently where the real restrictions were:

| Period | Phase | Product | Service |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| 18 Mar – 3 May 2020 | MCO 1.0 | 22 % | **0 %** |
| 4 May – 9 Jun 2020 | CMCO | 58 % | 30 % |
| 10 Jun – 13 Oct 2020 | RMCO | 90 % | 78 % |
| 14 Oct 2020 – 12 Jan 2021 | CMCO 2nd wave — **Selangor/WP only** (others stay RMCO) | 80 % | 58 % |
| 13 Jan – 4 Mar 2021 | MCO 2.0 | 48 % | 12 % |
| 5 Mar – 11 May 2021 | cautious recovery | 85 % | 70 % |
| 12 – 31 May 2021 | MCO 3.0 | 55 % | 25 % |
| 1 Jun – 31 Aug 2021 | FMCO (+ **EMCO 3–16 Jul in Selangor/WP**: 20 %) | 34 % | **0 %** |
| 1 Sep – 31 Oct 2021 | phased reopening (NRP) | 72 % | 48 % |
| 1 Nov 2021 – 31 Mar 2022 | transition to endemic | 95 % | 86 % |
| Apr 2022 onward | endemic | year growth takes over (2022 ×1.20 of 2019) | |

Also in the lockdown periods: the basket shifts to cleansers, masks and toners and away from serums,
sunscreen and eye cream; new customer registrations slow to a trickle; restocking volumes drop
because they follow units sold.

**Staff and payroll during COVID.** 12 % of junior therapists / sales assistants / cashiers resign
Apr–Jun 2020 and 10 % more Jul–Sep 2021 (`st_status = 'Resigned'`, salary stops, no transactions
after leaving); managers and senior therapists stay. −20 % pay Apr–May 2020, −15 % Jun–Aug 2021;
13th-month and Raya bonuses halved in 2020–2021; no annual increment in 2021; the EPF employee
deduction follows the real schedule (11 % → 7 % Apr–Dec 2020 → 9 % Jan 2021–Jun 2022 → 11 %).
The 2022 rehiring wave (30 people, Jan–Jun 2022) is in `data22_23/staff.csv`.

**Festivals.** Run-ups before Chinese New Year (×1.75 over 18 days), Hari Raya Aidilfitri (×1.85 over
25 days — the strongest, and it moves ~11 days earlier every year: 15 Jun 2018 … 31 Mar 2025),
Deepavali (×1.45 / 14 days), Christmas (×1.40 / 16 days); a dip on the holiday itself. Mega-sale
days 11.11 and 12.12 ×2.3, 3.3 / 9.9 / 10.10 ×1.5 (with 15 % promo discounts and bigger baskets),
Mega Sale Carnival mid-Jun–Aug ×1.06. Q4 strongest, Q3 weakest; Sat ×1.42, Sun ×1.25, Mon ×0.80.
The Raya bonus is paid in the Raya month, so it moves too (Jun 2018 → Mar 2025).

**Customers.** 33,947 registrations from March 2016 (branch openings) to December 2025, each with a
home branch. 80 % live in their branch's city, most of the rest elsewhere in the same state; 85 % of
a branch's orders come from its own customer pool, 15 % from a nearby branch's — so ~69 % of orders
are placed in the customer's own city. Every customer has an activity weight (Platinum > Gold >
Silver > Bronze) and either stays loyal or churns after a while, so regulars recur across all three
folders and new ones keep joining. Ages 18–61 at registration; tiers Bronze 55 / Silver 27 / Gold 13
/ Platinum 5 %; discounts 0 / 3 / 7 / 12 %.

**Reservations.** ~42 % of order volume in normal times. Slots 10:00–20:00 on a 15-minute grid,
peaking 16:00–18:00; each service has its own duration (add-ons 15–30 min, anti-ageing 90 min);
36 % add an add-on; extra 5 % off weekday mornings. Only therapists serve them, only at their own
branch, only while employed, and **never two bookings at once** — capacity is real, so fewer
therapists means fewer bookings. Non-therapists (sales assistants, cashiers, receptionists,
managers) take the product orders.

**Purchases.** Two restocking runs a month per branch (weekdays around the 3rd and 17th); each
product is reordered at ×1.08 the units the branch sold since its last restock plus 0–2 safety
units (rev 3; revision 2 used ×1.15 + 5–15), slow movers every other month, launch stock for new products, opening stock a week before
Ipoh opens. Supplier by brand; AuraDerm (7) and Nordic Skin (8) take some DermaVita / BotaniQ /
HydraLuxe volume from 2025. Unit cost 33–43 % of the shelf price *of that date* (rev 3; was 45–55 %).

**Money.** 6 % SST on the discounted amount — except 1 Jun–31 Aug 2018 (0 %). Rent by city tier
(KL 12.95k … Gombak 4.85k in 2018, 70 % of revision 2) +3 %/year, −30 % in the five closure months; electricity ∝ branch size,
+12 % in the hot months, near zero while closed.

**Price eras (the SCD2 test).** Product prices change on 2023-01-01 (7 products, in
[data22_23/99_price_increase_2023.sql](data22_23/99_price_increase_2023.sql)) and 2025-01-01
(8 products + 6 services, in [data24_25/99_price_change_2025.sql](data24_25/99_price_change_2025.sql)).
Every order/reservation line carries the price of its era: 85,279 lines for the uplifted products
at base prices, 49,968 at 2023 prices, 27,433 at 2025 prices. Products 4 and 16 end with three
versions in `product_dim` (63 rows = 48 current + 15 expired).

## Verified before shipping (`gen_sales_data3.py --verify`, 0 problems)

- headers identical to `sales_data\data`; every ID contiguous within a folder and continuing across folders
- no order or reservation before the customer's registration or the branch's opening date
- serving staff always at the transacting branch, employed on that date; reservations only by therapists
- no therapist double-booked; every appointment 10:00–20:00 with the service's exact duration
- **no reservation while salons were closed** (18 Mar–3 May 2020, 1 Jun–31 Aug 2021)
- no product / service / supplier used before its launch date; every line at the price of its era
- tax = 6 % of the discounted amount, 0 % only in the 2018 tax holiday; purchase cost 33–43 % of shelf
- salary rows only between hire and leave; EPF rate per month as scheduled
